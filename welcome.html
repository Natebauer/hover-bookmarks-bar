Move your mouse to the very top of this page

You're all set

Rest your mouse at the very top of any page, or keep moving it up past the top edge, and your bookmarks bar slides down. Move away and it tucks itself back up. Try it on this page.

  • Works in the tabs you already had openNo need to reload anything.
  • Turn off your browser's own bookmarks barIn most Chromium browsers that's Ctrl+Shift+B, so this bar is the only one on screen.
  • A few pages are off-limitsBrowsers block every extension on the Web Store, the New Tab page and settings pages, so the bar can't appear there.
  • Using Vivaldi?Choose the folder your Vivaldi bookmark bar shows, and the hover bar will show the same one.
